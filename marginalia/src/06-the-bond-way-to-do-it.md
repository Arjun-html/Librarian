---
title: "The Bond Way to Do It"
kicker: "Four rules from watching Bond"
---
![Daniel Craig as James Bond](images/bond.webp)

1. Bond stays clear, sticks to the plan he sets for himself.
2. Even when chasing a goon, he doesn't try to match the goon at what the goon's good at — instead, takes his own path.
3. Not afraid to take risks, as long as he knows his final goal.
4. Extremely good with technology; much of an engineer.
