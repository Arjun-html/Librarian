---
title: "Man, Am I Lucky"
kicker: "On Meditations"
---
When I was really young, my family used to grow crops and farm in our backyard, and I used to slog through plucking out all the weeds by hand, watering the rows and rows of crop after crop and generally have a foul mood doing the grunt work.

Watching Thorfinn do it as a form of repentance as he felt he had sinned so much made me so mad I had ever been angry in that field as a kid. I realized I have been the most privileged person I have ever met in my whole life.

I never had any major family issues, my parents put me into hobbies that are still considered cool, I was blessed with an above-average intelligence, gifted with height, no crazy illness — and my parents are sufficiently well off to even send me off to a foreign country with no scholarship.

I have not seen any true hardship in life, and that's why I think I connected with Marcus Aurelius so strongly. Why stress and fret over petty issues?
